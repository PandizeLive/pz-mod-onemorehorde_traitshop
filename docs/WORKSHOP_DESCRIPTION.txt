[h1]PRESTIGE[/h1]

By default, reaching Trait Levels [b]5, 10, 15[/b], and so on earns [b]1 Prestige Point[/b]. Each point gives your next character an extra trait point during character creation.

Your next character receives the full bonus, then retains [b]30% of that Prestige[/b] for future lives, rounded to the nearest whole point. Tokens, Trait Level, and XP reset on death.

[h1]HORDE NIGHT AND OFFICIAL SCORES[/h1]

With Horde Night installed, Trait Shop is enabled by default through [b]Enable Trait Shop[/b] in Run Options. Using purchased traits, removals, or a Prestige creation bonus keeps it enabled for the affected character or faction.

Trait Shop applies a [b]x0.50 score multiplier[/b], combined multiplicatively with other score modifiers. Runs can remain Official with Horde Night rewards and default-or-harder settings. Easier settings, enabling negative removal, or switching away from Horde Night reward mode make the affected run unofficial.

Characters created before Horde Night could verify their creation remain playable, but their runs are Unverified. Create a new character and reset the run for a fresh eligibility check. Fixing settings or respawning does not erase an old run's flags.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]GETTING STARTED AND COMPATIBILITY[/h1]

Enable Trait Shop, then hover over the health icon to reveal the shop button.

Common vanilla and modded traits are supported through their declared effects. Traits with custom scripts may need a compatibility patch. Zero-cost abilities and weight conditions appear in the read-only [b]Other Traits[/b] panel.
