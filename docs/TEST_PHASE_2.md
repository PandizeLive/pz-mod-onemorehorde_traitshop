1. Close the game, install through **OMH.cmd -> 1**, and launch through Steam. Use your disposable test save.
2. Move over the hidden shop-button area: nothing should appear. Hover health, move onto the revealed button, and open the shop.
3. Check the three panels, centered Trait Level, matching Tokens/Prestige totals and **0%** XP bar. Hover both totals for their breakdowns.
4. Search and cycle the filter. Prices should rise from cheapest to most expensive; unavailable traits stay grey and explain why. Clear the search and return to All traits.
5. Buy one affordable, unowned supported trait. It should move to your positive list and deduct the displayed cost. Current negatives should appear below with removal disabled.
6. Quit and reload. Confirm the trait and balance remain, then report any UI or purchase problems.
