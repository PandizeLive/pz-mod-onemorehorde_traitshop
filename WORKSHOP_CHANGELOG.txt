v0.6.3

Sandbox settings
- Added Trait Costs, XP Rewards, and Player Kill Rules section headers in solo, host, and live admin settings.
- Moved the player-kill cooldown below XP rewards. Its section hides in modes without player-kill rewards.

Level-up feedback
- Added a sound for Trait Level increases and a special sound when a level-up earns prestige.
- Added gold Trait Level Up! text above the player, with Token and Prestige rewards listed underneath, including while the shop is closed.
- Trait Level now starts at 1 with 0 / 100 XP toward Level 2. Existing progress is preserved.
- The starting level grants no Token or prestige. The first Prestige Point follows five earned level-ups.
- Loading, reconnecting and duplicate updates do not replay level-up feedback.

Still in development
- Horde rewards, prestige character-creation bonuses, and official scoring remain upcoming.
