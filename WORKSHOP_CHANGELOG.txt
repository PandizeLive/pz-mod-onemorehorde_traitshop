v0.6.4

Level Rewards
- Added Tokens per Level (default 1) and Prestige Level Interval (default 5) in a new Level Rewards sandbox section.
- Reward settings apply to future level-ups, preserving existing Tokens and Prestige.
- Prevented repeated interval changes from awarding the same Prestige entitlement again.
- Reward text and the Tokens tooltip now show actual Tokens earned with custom rewards.

Compatibility
- Update Horde Night to v0.6.13.1 for the matching official-settings policy.
- Prestige character-creation bonuses and retention remain Checkpoint 7.
