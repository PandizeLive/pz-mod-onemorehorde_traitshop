v0.8.1
Physical trait purchases
- Stout, Strong, Fit and Athletic now grant their Strength or Fitness increase, preserving existing XP progress and respecting level 10.
- Added stat-based removal of Puny, Weak, Unfit and Out of Shape when negative removal is enabled.
- Cart and trait tooltips preview the resulting stat and trait changes.
- Character-creation conflicts still apply; queue removal of a conflicting negative before buying its replacement.
- Physical grants apply once per purchase and do not repeatedly refill lost XP after reconnecting or restarting.
