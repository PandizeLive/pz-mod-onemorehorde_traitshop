v0.5.0

Checkpoint 5
- Added a read-only Other Traits panel between your positive and negative traits. Zero-cost traits, including profession abilities and weight conditions, appear here with description tooltips.
- Added the XP conversion foundation: one Token per earned Trait Level and one Prestige Point every five levels.
- Added gradual XP requirements, starting at 100 and reaching 2,000 for Level 20, rounded to the nearest 50.
- Updated the XP bar to show progress against the next level's requirement.
- Starting Tokens now capture unused points at character creation, protecting new characters against delayed first-join initialization.
- Added persistence and duplicate-award checks for progression and new-character grants.

Still in development
- XP earning sources, prestige character-creation bonuses, and Horde Night scoring integration. This update does not award XP for kills or survival yet.
