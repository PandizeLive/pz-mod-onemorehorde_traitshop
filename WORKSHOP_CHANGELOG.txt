Checkpoint 7 - Prestige
- Prestige now adds bonus points to your next character's trait selection.
- Added Prestige Retention Percentage, defaulting to 30%, in a new Prestige settings category.
- Unused creation points become Tokens; retained Prestige rounds to whole points without a minimum.
- Added persistence protections for canceled creation, repeated entry and reconnects.
- The Token tooltip now includes the Prestige creation bonus and its unused portion.
- Preserved the origin of inherited Prestige for Horde Night's official settings checks.
