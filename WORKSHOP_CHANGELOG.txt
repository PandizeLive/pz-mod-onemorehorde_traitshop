v0.8.0
Checkpoint 8 - Horde Night rewards
- Added 5 XP per credited Horde Zombie kill and 100 XP for surviving your completed horde by default.
- Hybrid rewards horde kills once, without also paying ordinary zombie XP.
- Casualties, disconnected players and unrelated players cannot claim horde completion rewards.
- Added persistent protection against repeated rewards after reconnects, respawns and restarts.
- Requires OMH: Horde Night 0.6.15 or newer for horde rewards. Standalone progression remains available.
